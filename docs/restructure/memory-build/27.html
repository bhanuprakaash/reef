Recall

Hybrid recall with link expansion

One call finds items by words and by meaning, adds their neighbours one link away, ranks them and cuts at a limit.

Issue #275981813c09ae25memory-servicebased on docs/restructure-proposal

What it does

Recall(ctx, Query{Scopes, Text, Limit, Scope}) runs Search and Nearest over the asker's shelves. It fuses the two ranked lists by reciprocal rank fusion: each list adds 1/(60 + rank) to an item's score. From each of the best hits it follows links one step: links to other items, links that point at the hit, and other items about the same target. Targets of kind project or team are hubs and are never followed. Each hit says how it was reached in Via, for example direct or replaces memory:1.

Why

Decision R19: word search and meaning search cover each other's gaps, and one link step brings the neighbourhood without the whole project. R18: recall may return retired items, marked retired, so the history of what was believed stays readable.

Decisions made while building

Rename to Match

The ticket names the recall result Hit, so the search result became Match.

Newer first on ties

RRF ranks are always distinct, so the lists themselves order equal scores accepted first, then newer first. That makes the newest accepted of two conflicting findings rank first.

Embed failure

If Nearest fails, recall answers from words only. Embeddings are optional (R20).

Higher score wins

An item reached twice keeps its higher score. A weak direct hit can then show the link it was also reached through.

Review fix: neighbour score

A neighbour scores s/(1+s): the RRF score one rank below its parent. Half the parent's score never beat direct rank 50, so expansion vanished once ten items matched.
