Embeddings

Gemini embedding provider

Google Gemini is a third embedding provider. It is free to try, and it has its own measured cosine floor.

Issue #336ce3a8a28903eb82edfd6memory-servicebased on docs/restructure-proposal

What it does

reef memory embed gemini --key K stores Gemini as the provider. geminiEmbedder calls gemini-embedding-001 with the key in the x-goog-api-key header and asks for 768 numbers. Each embedder now reports MinCosine: the similarity under which a meaning match is unrelated. Gemini uses 0.6; OpenAI and Voyage keep 0.3. The eval gives the embedder to every scenario when a key is set, not only the synonym ones.

Why

R28: Gemini has a free tier, so meaning search costs nothing to try. The real eval showed that one floor cannot fit every model: with 0.3, Gemini let unrelated notes into 13 of 29 scenarios.

Decisions made while building

No task type

Measured with the real API: RETRIEVAL_QUERY/RETRIEVAL_DOCUMENT and SEMANTIC_SIMILARITY raise every score but do not widen the gap between related and unrelated notes. So the request sends none, and the Embedder interface stays one-way.

Floor 0.6 for Gemini

Related notes scored 0.685 to 0.758; unrelated at most 0.569. 0.6 sits in the gap. OpenAI and Voyage stay 0.3 until someone runs the eval with their keys.

Lookalike stays 0.92

Near-duplicates scored 0.98; reworded notes 0.84 to 0.87; unrelated 0.52. Only near-copies are flagged.
